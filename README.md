# How I Approach Projects

I use a structured problem-solving approach:

### 1. Understand

Identify the business problem, stakeholders, objectives, constraints, and desired outcomes.

### 2. Analyze

Examine the available data, processes, systems, and potential root causes.

### 3. Design

Develop a practical solution architecture that aligns technology capabilities with business requirements.

### 4. Build

Develop the analytics, automation, AI, data integration, or reporting solution.

### 5. Validate

Test the solution, validate data quality, and confirm that the output meets business requirements.

### 6. Measure

Compare the process before and after implementation using measurable business outcomes.

### 7. Improve

Identify opportunities to optimize, automate further, and scale the solution.

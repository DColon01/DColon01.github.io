Data Quality & Analytics

I'm currently developing this case study to demonstrate how data analysis, data validation, and business intelligence can be used to identify discrepancies, uncover root causes, and improve decision-making.

The completed project will include the business problem, analytical approach, data-quality methodology, solution design, technologies used, and measurable business impact.

What This Project Will Demonstrate

Data Quality

Identifying missing, inconsistent, duplicate, or inaccurate data and developing methods to validate and improve data quality.

Root Cause Analysis

Investigating discrepancies and identifying the underlying causes rather than simply correcting individual data issues.

Analytics

Transforming raw information into meaningful metrics, trends, patterns, and actionable insights.

Business Intelligence

Using dashboards and visualizations to help stakeholders understand data quality and operational performance.

Planned Technology Stack

SQL Power BI Python Data Analysis Data Quality Data Validation Data Visualization

Planned Project Approach

01 — Discover

Define the business problem and understand the impact of inaccurate or inconsistent data.

02 — Analyze

Explore the dataset, identify patterns, and investigate potential data-quality issues.

03 — Validate

Develop validation rules and analytical checks to identify discrepancies.

04 — Visualize

Build dashboards and visualizations that make data-quality issues easy to understand.

05 — Recommend

Translate analytical findings into practical recommendations and process improvements.

06 — Measure

Quantify the impact of the improvements and identify opportunities for continued optimization.

Project Overview

Designed and developed an end-to-end AI-powered reporting automation solution to reduce the time and manual effort required to produce recurring weekly status reports.

The solution combined Microsoft Power Automate, AI Builder custom prompts, enterprise data sources, Power BI, SharePoint, and automated document generation to transform a repetitive reporting process into a streamlined data-driven workflow.

```
```

Business Problem

The Challenge

Weekly reporting required significant manual effort. Information had to be gathered from multiple sources, reviewed, interpreted, formatted, and incorporated into a standardized status report.

The process was time-consuming and created opportunities for inconsistent formatting, manual errors, and delays in distributing information to stakeholders.

```
```

Project Objectives

01. Reduce Manual Work

Automate repetitive activities involved in collecting, processing, and formatting reporting information.

02. Improve Accuracy

Reduce opportunities for manual data-entry and reporting errors.

03. Accelerate Reporting

Reduce the time required to produce recurring weekly status reports.

04. Enable Scalability

Create a repeatable solution that could potentially be extended to additional teams and reporting processes.

Solution Architecture

The solution was designed as an end-to-end workflow that connected data ingestion, AI processing, business logic, reporting, and document generation.

High-Level Workflow

Data Sources → Data Warehouse → Power Automate → AI Builder / Custom Prompts → Business Logic → Power BI / SharePoint → Automated Report

1. Data Sources

Collect information required for the weekly reporting process from available enterprise data sources.

2. Data Processing

Prepare and organize information for downstream analytics and automation.

3. AI Processing

Use AI Builder and custom prompts to interpret and extract useful information from unstructured content.

4. Workflow Automation

Power Automate coordinates the workflow and moves information between systems.

5. Reporting

Power BI provides analytical reporting and visualization capabilities.

6. Document Generation

Processed information is incorporated into standardized reporting documents.

```
```

AI Component

A key component of the solution was the use of AI Builder and custom prompts to process information that would otherwise require manual review and interpretation.

AI Processing Approach

  1. Receive source information.
  2. Submit relevant content to the AI processing step.
  3. Apply a structured prompt to identify and organize the required information.
  4. Validate the returned information against expected business requirements.
  5. Pass the structured output to downstream workflow steps.
  6. Generate the appropriate reporting output.
```
```

Automation Workflow

Step 1 — Trigger

The workflow is initiated based on the reporting schedule or designated business event.

Step 2 — Retrieve Data

Required reporting information is retrieved from connected enterprise data sources.

Step 3 — Process Information

Data and unstructured content are processed using workflow logic and AI capabilities.

Step 4 — Generate Output

The workflow transforms the processed information into the required reporting format.

Step 5 — Distribute

The completed report can be stored and distributed through the organization's approved collaboration and document-management environment.

Lessons Learned

The project demonstrated that successful AI adoption is not simply about introducing an AI model. The greatest value came from integrating AI into an existing business process and connecting it to automation, data, reporting, and document-management capabilities.

Establishing clear business rules, validating AI output, and measuring the process before and after automation were important to demonstrating the solution's value.

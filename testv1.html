Executive summary

Less manual effort. More operational value.

An AI-powered workflow that converts unstructured document content into structured, validated, and formatted CARA reports with significantly less manual intervention.

The business challenge

Preparing CARA spreadsheets required manually reviewing source PDFs or images, identifying relevant information, transferring data into Excel templates, formatting outputs, and distributing completed reports.

  • ! Repetitive data identification and spreadsheet entry.
  • ! Manual formatting and preparation of standardized reports.
  • ! Potential for transcription errors, rework, and inconsistent outputs.

The solution

A Microsoft Power Automate workflow integrates AI Builder Text Recognition, bounding-box-based content identification, SharePoint-hosted Excel templates, Office Scripts, validation, exception handling, notifications, and email delivery.

  • ✓ Extract document text and identify relevant content dynamically.
  • ✓ Populate and format the target spreadsheet automatically.
  • ✓ Validate data and notify stakeholders of completion or exceptions.

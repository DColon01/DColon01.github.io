Executive summary

From manual reporting to automated intelligence.

A deployed reporting pipeline that combines AI-assisted content generation, enterprise data, analytics, and collaboration tools to reduce the effort required to produce weekly status reports.

The challenge

Weekly status reporting for the [XYZ] program required approximately four hours of effort. Recurring report preparation consumed valuable team capacity that could otherwise support program delivery, analysis, and stakeholder engagement.

  • !Time-intensive weekly report preparation.
  • !Need to bring together relevant enterprise data and reporting context.
  • !Opportunity to standardize reporting and improve operational scalability.

The solution

An AI-powered reporting pipeline orchestrated through Power Automate. AI Builder custom prompts support report-content generation, while Data Warehouse and Power BI provide the supporting data and analytics ecosystem, with SharePoint supporting report collaboration and access.

  • ✓Automates recurring reporting activities through an orchestrated pipeline.
  • ✓Reduces generation time from four hours to under 30 minutes.
  • ✓Establishes a foundation for potential enterprise-wide adoption.
